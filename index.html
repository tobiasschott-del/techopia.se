Ways to work with us

How we work

We work close to your leadership team, from understanding the business to a signed agreement.

  1. We get to know your business

    We work closely with the management team of your tech company to understand the business, the product development and the portfolio.

  2. We define the profile

    Together we pin down the requirements: what the role is, and what it is meant to deliver.

  3. We search and identify

    We find individuals who match, from our own network or by approaching people we haven’t met before.

  4. We build the relationship

    We make contact, build trust, pitch the opportunity and listen to what the person needs.

  5. We close it, your way

    The last steps follow your preference.

    We run the full process

    We handle the quality assurance and the whole recruitment process for you.

    We pair up with your team

    Your internal HR or recruitment team handles quality assurance, references and the like, and we work alongside them.

Every step is confidential by default. Your company name stays out of it until you decide otherwise.
