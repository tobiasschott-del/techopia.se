How a search works

  1. We agree on the role

    What the person will own, who they’ll work with, and what success looks like.

  2. We map and approach

    We find the right people across the market and reach out discreetly, one by one.

  3. You meet a short list

    A few candidates worth your time, with our assessment of each.

  4. We close it together

    We support both sides through to a signed agreement.

Every step is confidential by default. Your company name stays out of it until you decide otherwise.
