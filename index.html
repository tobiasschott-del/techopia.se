Discretion is the service.

The roles we fill are rarely public, and the people we approach are rarely looking. How a search is handled matters as much as who it finds.

  • Integrity

    We act in the interest of your company and of every person we approach, and we are straightforward with both.

  • Discretion

    Your company name stays out of it until you decide otherwise. Only the people you designate know a search exists.

  • Confidentiality

    We work under NDA with you, and candidates sign one too before we share any details.

Secret search and proactive pipelining

Our expertise is recruitment of key positions, including confidential hirings, secret sourcing and proactive talent pipelining for succession planning.

  • Secret search

    For roles that must not be visible: succeeding a sitting leader, hiring ahead of an announcement, recruiting from a competitor. We source in silence, never advertise, and approach candidates directly.

  • Proactive talent pipelining

    We build relationships with the people your company will need next, before a seat opens. When a leader leaves or the business changes direction, you already know who to call.

Ways to work with us

Whether you need a key hire now or want to be ready for the next one, we work in the format that fits.

  • Pay-per-hire

    Suited for recruiting key positions on permanent employment contracts, in your organisation's long-term interest. You pay for each hire we make.

  • Strategic talent readiness

    An ongoing partnership where we build talent pipelines for your key roles. We meet monthly to review the pipeline, and approach profiles when you say go. Built for staying ahead, succession planning and organisational change.

How we work

We work close to your leadership team, from understanding the business to a signed agreement.

Every step is confidential by default. Your company name stays out of it until you decide otherwise.

  1. We get to know your business

    We work closely with the management team of your tech company to understand the business, the product development and the portfolio.

  2. We define the profile

    Together we pin down the requirements: what the role is, and what it is meant to deliver.

  3. We search and identify

    We find individuals who match, from our own network or by approaching people we haven’t met before.

  4. We build the relationship

    We make contact, build trust, pitch the opportunity and listen to what the person needs.

  5. We close it, your way

    The last steps follow your preference.

    We run the full process

    We handle the quality assurance and the whole recruitment process for you.

    We pair up with your team

    Your internal HR or recruitment team handles quality assurance, references and the like, and we work alongside them.
