Ways to work with us

We provide the expertise in the contract format that fits your company best.

  • Pay-per-hire

    You pay for each hire we make. It suits replacement hires, and recruiting key people in small numbers.

  • Experts inside your talent team

    Hire us in as experts for your talent acquisition or sourcing team. It suits scaleups that need to put a team of key people in place, or build a sourcing strategy and function from the ground up.

  • Interim and brokered talent

    When you have a temporary need for key people, including interim leaders, you hire them through us. We act as the broker, and you have the expertise for as long as you need it.

How a search works

  1. We agree on the role

    What the person will own, who they’ll work with, and what success looks like.

  2. We map and approach

    We find the right people across the market and reach out discreetly, one by one.

  3. You meet a short list

    A few candidates worth your time, with our assessment of each.

  4. We close it together

    We support both sides through to a signed agreement.

Every step is confidential by default. Your company name stays out of it until you decide otherwise.
